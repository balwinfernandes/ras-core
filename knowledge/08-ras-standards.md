## RAS Standing Committee for Standards

The IEEE RAS Standing Committee for Standards develops IEEE standards for robotics and automation. Its published standards include:

- IEEE 1872-2015: Standard Ontologies for Robotics and Automation.
- IEEE 1873-2015: Standard for Robot Map Data Representation for Navigation.
- IEEE 1872.2-2021: Standard for Autonomous Robotics (AuR) Ontology.
- IEEE 7007-2021: Ontological Standard for Ethically Driven Robotics and Automation Systems.

These standards give robots and robotics software a shared vocabulary for knowledge, maps, autonomy and ethics, so systems from different developers can work together.
