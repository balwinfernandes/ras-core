## ICRA — International Conference on Robotics and Automation

ICRA is the IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation, the flagship conference fully sponsored by IEEE RAS. It brings together researchers and companies for paper sessions, panels, tutorials, workshops and exhibits.

ICRA 2026 was held from June 1 to June 5, 2026 at VIECON, the Vienna Congress and Convention Center in Vienna, Austria.

ICRA 2027 will be held in Seoul, Republic of Korea, from May 24 to May 28, 2027. Paper submissions opened on July 15, 2026 and the paper submission deadline was September 15, 2026. Workshop and tutorial pre-proposals were due September 1, 2026, with full proposals due November 1, 2026. Acceptance notifications are expected on January 31, 2027 and final papers are due March 6, 2027.

## IROS — International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems

IROS is the IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems. RAS financially co-sponsors it, and it ranks alongside ICRA as one of the premier robotics conferences. It has been held every year since 1988, when the first IROS took place at the Tokyo University of Science in Japan. Recent and upcoming hosts: 2023 Detroit (USA), 2024 Abu Dhabi (UAE), 2025 Hangzhou (China), 2026 Pittsburgh (USA), 2027 Florence (Italy).

## CASE — Conference on Automation Science and Engineering

CASE is the IEEE International Conference on Automation Science and Engineering, the fully sponsored RAS flagship conference for automation. CASE 2026, the 22nd edition, was held in Shenyang, China, in August 2026.

## Other fully sponsored RAS conferences

Besides ICRA and CASE, RAS fully sponsors:
- Humanoids (IEEE-RAS International Conference on Humanoid Robots). The 2026 event is the 25th edition.
- RoboSoft (IEEE International Conference on Soft Robotics). RoboSoft 2026 was hosted in Kanazawa, Japan.
- HAPTICS (IEEE Haptics Symposium) and WHC (IEEE World Haptics Conference).
- SSRR (Safety, Security and Rescue Robotics).
- ICAR (Advanced Robotics).
- ARSO (Advanced Robotics and its Social Impacts).
- MRS (Multi-Robot and Multi-Agent Systems).
- CBS (Cyborg and Bionic Systems).
- ERAS (Engineering Reliable Autonomous Systems).

RAS also financially co-sponsors and technically co-sponsors many more conferences and workshops around the world.
