## Membership benefits

IEEE RAS membership benefits include:

- Online access to IEEE Robotics and Automation Magazine, IEEE Transactions on Robotics (T-RO), IEEE Transactions on Automation Science and Engineering (T-ASE) and IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters (RA-L).
- Access to the IEEE Xplore digital library and the RAS Digital Conference Library.
- The RAS Resource Center, with conference videos and expert lectures.
- Membership in local chapters, which operate in over 40 countries.
- Participation in more than 40 technical committees.
- Discounts and an industry conference calendar covering ICRA, CASE and IROS.

## How to join

You join RAS through IEEE. The official "Join the Robotics and Automation Society" link on ieee-ras.org leads to the IEEE membership catalog entry for RAS (product MEMRA024), which is used for both joining and renewing. General IEEE membership is available at ieee.org/join.

## Students

The RAS Student Activities Committee (SAC) organises activities for student members, especially at RAS conferences, such as student social gatherings and team competitions. Students can also get involved through their university's RAS student branch chapter, or start one through IEEE's petition process.
