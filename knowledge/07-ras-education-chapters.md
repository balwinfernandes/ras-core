## Distinguished Lecturer Program

The RAS Distinguished Lecturer Program lets IEEE sections, student branch chapters and other organisations invite renowned robotics experts to speak. RAS provides travel support of up to 5,000 USD for the lecturer, with priority for chapters that have not received funding before. Distinguished Lecturers are nominated by technical committees and serve three-year terms, which helps chapters without regular access to international speakers.

## Other education programmes

The RAS Educational Activities Board oversees summer schools, competitions, pre-college resources, technical education programmes, RAS University learning resources, CEMRA and ICRA-X. It also develops new initiatives for pre-university education and engagement.

## Chapters worldwide

RAS has more than 220 local and regional chapters across IEEE's regions. Chapters sponsor or co-sponsor mini-symposia, student competitions and continuing-education workshops, which is especially valuable for engineers with few robotics colleagues nearby. Local chapters operate within IEEE sections; student branch chapters are affiliated with university IEEE student branches and have a faculty advisor. New chapters are formed through IEEE's petition process.

## Chapter funding

- Chapter Start-Up Grant: 500 USD.
- Chapter Initiative Grant: up to 2,000 USD.
- Chapter of the Year awards recognise outstanding section chapters and student branch chapters.
