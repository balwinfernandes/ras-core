## What IEEE RAS is

The IEEE Robotics and Automation Society (IEEE RAS) is IEEE's global professional society for robotics and automation. Its mission is to "foster the development and facilitate the exchange of scientific and technological knowledge in Robotics and Automation that benefits members, the profession and humanity."

Its vision is to "be the most recognized and respected global organization in Robotics and Automation."

## Field of interest

On the robotics side, RAS covers robotic systems that use sensors and actuators to operate autonomously or semi-autonomously, often in cooperation with humans, with an emphasis on intelligence and the ability to adapt to the environment.

On the automation side, RAS focuses on the efficiency, productivity, quality and reliability of autonomous systems, particularly those working in structured environments such as factories.

## History

- 1984: the Robotics and Automation Council (RAC) was founded under the leadership of George Saridis. Eight existing IEEE societies, including the Control Systems Society and the Computer Society, came together to form it.
- 1985: the IEEE Journal of Robotics and Automation began publishing.
- 1987: the council became a full IEEE Society, the IEEE Robotics and Automation Society.
- 1988: the first IEEE/RSJ IROS conference was held at the Tokyo University of Science, Japan.
- 1989: the journal was renamed IEEE Transactions on Robotics and Automation.
- 2004: the journal split, and its robotics side continued as IEEE Transactions on Robotics (T-RO).
- June 1, 2015: IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters (RA-L) launched.
- 2026: ICRA was held in Vienna, Austria. ICRA 2027 will be held in Seoul, Republic of Korea.

## Size and reach

RAS has over 19,000 members around the world. It has more than 220 local and student branch chapters across IEEE's regions, with members in more than 40 countries, and 47 technical committees.

## Leadership

The IEEE RAS Executive Committee lists Nancy Amato (University of Illinois Urbana) as President and Kyu-Jin Cho (Seoul National University) as President-Elect. The Junior Past President is Aude Billard (EPFL), and the Senior Past President is Frank Park (Seoul National University).

RAS is governed by an Executive Committee and an Administrative Committee (AdCom), with boards for conferences, publications, educational activities, technical activities and member activities.

## What RAS does

RAS publishes journals and magazines, runs and sponsors conferences worldwide, connects members through technical committees and chapters, runs education programmes, recognises achievement through awards, and develops IEEE standards for robotics.
