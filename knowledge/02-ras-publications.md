## Overview of RAS journals

IEEE RAS publishes and co-sponsors ten journals and magazines. Titles published by RAS: T-RO, RA-L, T-ASE, RA-M, RA-P, T-FR and the newer T-SRO. Co-sponsored titles: T-RL, ToH and T-MRB. RAS members get online access to key titles such as RA-M, T-RO, T-ASE and RA-L, plus discounted subscription rates.

## T-RO — IEEE Transactions on Robotics

T-RO publishes research papers that represent major advances in the state of the art in all areas of robotics, combining theory, design, experiments, algorithms and applications. Its ieee-ras.org page lists a JCR Impact Factor of 11.1 (2025), and open-access publication is available. It traces back to the IEEE Journal of Robotics and Automation (1985), which became IEEE Transactions on Robotics and Automation in 1989 and IEEE Transactions on Robotics in 2004.

## RA-L — IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters

RA-L launched on June 1, 2015. It is an electronic-only journal built for rapid publication: papers are six pages, with up to two extra pages allowed at a charge, and most papers get a decision within 6 months. Accepted RA-L papers can be presented at ICRA, IROS or CASE within 270 days of acceptance.

## T-ASE — IEEE Transactions on Automation Science and Engineering

T-ASE covers automation science and engineering. It is the Society's main journal for automation research.

## RA-M — IEEE Robotics and Automation Magazine

RA-M is the Society's flagship magazine, published quarterly in March, June, September and December. It presents high-impact research articles in an engaging, accessible style, along with reviews, columns and opinion pieces, and bridges academic journals and industry publications. Website: ramagazine.ieee.org.

## RA-P — IEEE Robotics and Automation Practice

RA-P launched in December 2024. It publishes applied research from practitioners with real-world deployments, in a short format: up to four pages, plus one page for supplementary materials and one page for references (4+1+1).

## T-FR — IEEE Transactions on Field Robotics

T-FR is dedicated to field robotics, meaning robots working outside controlled environments. Its paper archive is freely accessible (open access) on IEEE Xplore.

## T-SRO — IEEE Transactions on Soft Robotics

T-SRO is a newer RAS journal publishing fundamental advances in soft robotics, covering robotic systems with structural compliance: materials, sensors, actuators, design, control and AI-driven approaches. It uses double-anonymous review, and accepted papers may be presented at RoboSoft.

## Co-sponsored journals

- T-RL (IEEE Transactions on Robot Learning) covers fundamental advances in AI methods that address challenges specific to robotic and automation systems, including transfer learning, safe AI-based control, human-robot collaboration and reproducible benchmarks.
- ToH (IEEE Transactions on Haptics) covers haptic systems and touch feedback.
- T-MRB (IEEE Transactions on Medical Robotics and Bionics) covers medical robotics and bionics research.
