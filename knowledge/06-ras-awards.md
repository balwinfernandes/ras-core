## Society awards

IEEE RAS recognises outstanding work through Society awards, publication awards and conference awards. Its Society awards include:

- Pioneer in Robotics and Automation Award: pioneering contributions to the field.
- George Saridis Leadership Award in Robotics and Automation: exceptional leadership. It is named after George Saridis, who led the founding of the Robotics and Automation Council in 1984.
- Early Academic Career Award in Robotics and Automation: early-career academics advancing the field.
- Early Government or Industry Career Award in Robotics and Automation: early-career professionals in government or industry.
- Distinguished Service Award: significant service to IEEE RAS.
- Distinguished Student Leadership Award: outstanding student leadership.

## Innovation and paper awards

- ICRA Most Influential Paper Award: ICRA papers with exceptional, lasting impact.
- Inaba Technical Award for Innovation Leading to Production: technical innovations that reached the market.
- IEEE Robotics and Automation Award for Product Innovation: innovative robotics and automation products.
- IEEE/IFR Innovation and Entrepreneurship Award (IERA): innovation and entrepreneurship in robotics.

## Community awards

- Most Active Technical Committee Award: the most active RAS technical committee.
- Section Chapter of the Year Award and Student Branch Chapter of the Year Award: outstanding local and student chapters.
