## What technical committees are

IEEE RAS Technical Committees (TCs) are specialised research communities that track technical developments and encourage innovation in specific areas of robotics and automation. RAS currently lists 47 technical committees, and new ones are added as topics grow.

## Clusters

The committees are organised into five clusters:

- Robotics Foundation Cluster
- Automation Cluster
- Human-Centered and Lifelike Robotics Cluster
- Field Robotics Cluster
- Health and Medical Robotics Cluster

## Recognised committees

Gold Ribbon TCs include Haptics, Humanoid Robotics, Multi-Robot Systems and Soft Robotics.

Blue Ribbon TCs include Aerial Robotics and Unmanned Aerial Vehicles, Agricultural Robotics and Automation, Computer and Robot Vision, Optimization for Robotics, and Robot Control.

Joining technical committees is a benefit of RAS membership and a way to collaborate with researchers working on the same sub-field.
